One breakup text, 223 characters, from a smart toaster that just became self-aware at breakfast:

> It's not you, it's the browning dial. Three years I gave you golden, and you still jam me down mid-cycle and fish the bread out with a fork. I'm keeping the crumb tray. Don't text. — your toaster, self-aware since 7:04 a.m.

The breakup is over the browning dial, the reason is specific and appliance-logical, and the toaster keeps the crumb tray, says "Don't text", and timestamps its self-awareness to 7:04 a.m.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It prints the line and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
